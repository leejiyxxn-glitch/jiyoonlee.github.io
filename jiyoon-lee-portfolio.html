01 — About

Who I am

"Fashion is my passion — the marketing behind it, my curiosity."

Fashion is my big passion, and I've always been curious about the marketing behind my favorite brands. I want to grow into a marketer who can share cool styles with the world.

I sit at the intersection of aesthetic intuition and analytical thinking — studying how language, visuals, and brand voice work together to define what feels "trendy" in Korean fashion culture.

The Language of Fashion Dashboard

Analyzing How Korean Fashion Brands Construct Trendiness Through Words

An analytical project exploring the linguistic strategies Korean fashion brands use to communicate trendiness. By examining word choice, tone, and semantic patterns across brand copy, social media, and campaign messaging, this dashboard surfaces what "cool" really means in the Korean fashion lexicon.
